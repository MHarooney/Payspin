# Payspin — Executive Summary (Investor-Facing)

**What:** A **non-custodial** SEPA-Instant **payment-link + savings-circle (ROSCA)** fintech for the
Netherlands and Germany. Payments are initiated bank-to-bank via a PISP (**Yapily** now, **IbanXS** DNB-licensed
backup) through a swappable `PisProvider` layer — so Payspin **never holds funds** and stays outside BaFin/MiCA
e-money/banking licensing. A flat-fee, non-interest model with an **Islamic legal opinion (fatwa)** anchors an
ethical-finance / diaspora positioning that incumbents ignore.

**Three lines:** consumer payment links, business payment links, ROSCA "Circles".

**The thesis, stated honestly:**
- **Consumer links are a viral funnel, not revenue** — P2P "request money" is free at Tikkie, banks and PayPal.me.
- **ROSCA is the wedge** — sticky, low-CAC (community-led), differentiated (flat-fee, ethical, fatwa-backed),
  and live in **Germany where Tikkie doesn't operate**.
- **Business links are the only profit engine** — and must scale to **~2,600 accounts (or ~€35 ARPA)** to break even.

**Financials (base case, conservative, EUR):**

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | €1.6k | €23.5k | €117.4k |
| Net | −€75k | −€209k | −€268k |
| Exit MRR | — | — | ~€13k |

- **Monthly break-even is NOT reached within 36 months** at base-case caps; the upside case reaches it ~M37–38,
  driven entirely by the business stream. Cash tax ≈ €0 (loss carry-forward).
- **Peak funding need:** ~€0.55M within 36 months; **~€0.8–1.2M** to fund through business-led break-even.
- **Yapily-independence would cost €0.6–1.2M one-time + €0.25–0.4M/yr — more than the whole plan. Do not do it;**
  stay on PISP-aggregation with Yapily+IbanXS redundancy. That regulatory-light structure is the core advantage.

**Hidden risks flagged:** NL **DGA minimum salary €58k/yr** (deferred in the model — lands at profitability);
the **€300/mo CTO is non-market** and ramps to ~€9k/mo with a 2nd hire; **MiCA exposure** if Phase-3 crypto rails
are adopted too early.

**Verdict — fundable, as pre-seed.** Not on consumer unit economics; yes on the ROSCA/ethical/diaspora wedge,
the resilience story, and the Phase-3 cost collapse. **Investor profile:** pre-seed fintech / ethical-finance /
diaspora angels → fintech seed fund after business MRR is proven.

**Ask: €400k pre-seed (18-month runway).** Use of funds: **60% product/engineering** (business-link tooling:
recurring collection, B2B/marketplace payouts; ROSCA ops), **25% GTM** (NL/DE diaspora + SME acquisition),
**15% compliance/legal + buffer.** Raise the seed (€1.0–1.5M) only once business MRR ≈ €8–15k with LTV:CAC > 2.5.
